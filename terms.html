2. Description of service

Current is a WhatsApp message subscription operated by Premrajka LLC. Once subscribed, you choose one or more topics — currently AI, Robotics, Tech Gossip, Startup Finance, and Space Tech — and receive a short message every morning spotlighting one thing in the news, usually a product, a company, a funding round, or something a tech leader said or did publicly, with a plain-language explanation and why it matters today.
